# Reference analysis: principles, not clones

Method: the `getdesign` skill's capture and token workflow. Captures were taken at 1440×900 (hero and one scrolled viewport, saved in `images/`), and computed styles were read in the browser on 23 Sep 2026. The brief forbids cloning, so this is **not** a full 9-section design.md per site. It records only the grounded tokens needed to understand each site's mechanics, plus the principle ALTTO takes from it.

## What each one teaches, and what ALTTO does with it

**SEM: photography as the page, text as a caption.**
- Seen: a full-bleed plate with type set small and low. Nav links are spread across the width instead of clustered. Images come in different proportions, and text and image blocks sit off-grid from one another.
- Taken: photographs at unequal sizes that break the grid. Atmosphere frames bleed off both edges.
- Refused: its palette (teal / terracotta), its sans-only voice, and the award-logo row. ALTTO has no awards to show.

**Khufu's: night, one light source, one idea per viewport.**
- Seen: a dark field with candle-points of warm light, one underlined text CTA, and a script word that inflects the caps line.
- Taken: the "one warm lamp" thesis. Brass appears only where light lands. There is one underlined CTA per group, and one inflected (italic) word per chapter.
- Refused: navy, illustration, arch masks and uppercase display. ALTTO is more casual and social.

**Mujō: restraint and few elements per viewport.**
- Seen: the photo dimmed to an environment, tracked uppercase nav, almost nothing else.
- Taken: small tracked nav labels, few competing elements, and dark photography that becomes the ground.
- Refused: pure #000 (ALTTO uses warm #110d0a), Japanese cultural signs, omakase and Michelin positioning, and outlined boxy buttons.

**Noma: editorial confidence, invisible interface.**
- Seen: a large photo at half width against a reading column that starts off-centre, generous leading, and nav set as words.
- Taken: asymmetric image/text pairs (Experience chapter), serif-led hierarchy, UI that is only type.
- Refused: the light cream ground and the newsletter/cookie chrome.

**Le Sya (secondary): dark hospitality pacing.**
- Seen: gold tracked caps over interior photography.
- Taken: only the confidence to let one expanded word carry a chapter ("DRINKS" in Archivo wdth 125).
- Refused: gold-caps luxury and interior-as-hero. The ALTTO skill explicitly rejects "serifs douradas" corporate luxury.

## Combined principle

SEM's unequal photo rhythm, Khufu's single warm light, Mujō's reduction, Noma's type-led calm, translated into **a dark table under one warm lamp** for a social sushi rodízio in Lisbon. The result must look like ALTTO, not like a collage of the references. See `.impeccable/surfaces/index-html.md` (direction contract) and `docs/design-system/`.
