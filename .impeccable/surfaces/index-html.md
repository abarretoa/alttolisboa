## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a dark table seen under one warm lamp — every chapter is lit, not boxed. It refuses the category default of "hero photo + three feature cards + pricing tiers + testimonial slider".

OWN-WORLD: Warm slate near-black (#110d0a family) as the table; warm off-white type; brass used only where light would fall (a price, an underline, a hairline catching light). Fraunces at optical sizes for display — upright roman for statements, italic only for one word per chapter; Archivo (width axis) for labels and data. No radius, no containers, hairlines at 12% opacity. Photographs are cropped hard, dark-edged, placed at unequal sizes that break the grid.

STORY: Visitor sees the phrase and one plate under light → understands rodízio + mood → reads three prices like a menu card → sees how an evening there feels → trusts 4,7 on Google → gets address, hours, call, directions.
